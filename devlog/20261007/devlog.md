Shark Attackers of SPLORR!! now saves, because the sharks do not

Yer boat has been rebuilt, and it looks exactly the same.

I rewrote the whole game in Odin and compiled it to WebAssembly. It is the same menus, the same words and the same sea. The jam version was VB.NET, and now it is a small Odin program that runs in yer browser with a bit of JavaScript to put buttons on the page.

If you played it before, you will not notice anything until you close the tab.

What's new:

- The game saves. Every move is written down in yer browser, so you can close the tab, come back, and land right where you were: out at sea, in the middle of a fight, or in the middle of an ad break with the clock still running. If you were dead, you are still dead. Abandon and it forgets you.
- You have to give yer name. The old version would take no name at all and call you nothing. That seemed like too much freedom.

What's the same: the pier, the boat, the heading and the speed, the sharks and the way the fight ends. You still cannot win the fight. That is the point of the metaphor.

The sponsored ad breaks are still there too, still two minutes, and umlaut.fyi and Pen 15 are still very glad you are here.

One thing that is going away: the Windows, Linux and Mac console downloads. The browser version is the one I'll keep updated, so the old downloads are being retired.

I wrote this with Claude Code, which did most of the typing.
